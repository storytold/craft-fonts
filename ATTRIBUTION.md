The OFL allows these fonts to be bundled, embedded and redistributed with software, provided the
licence text travels with them and the fonts are not sold on their own. The family names are the
authors' names for their fonts, and some are Reserved Font Names under the OFL (IBM Plex: "Plex";
Reem Kufi; Aref Ruqaa). We use the files unmodified, so the reservations don't restrict us. A
modified version would have to be renamed.
